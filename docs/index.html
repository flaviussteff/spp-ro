01 // THE SCIENTIFIC PROBLEM
Theoretical Motivation

The Superficial Alignment Hypothesis vs. Model Raising

Do conventional post-hoc alignment techniques (SFT, RLHF, DPO, LoRA) eliminate deep representational stereotypes, or do they merely superimpose a fragile behavioral mask over pre-training weights?

Conventional Post-Hoc Alignment

Superficial Filter (SFT / RLHF / LoRA)

The model is pre-trained on uncurated web crawls, internalizing cultural prejudices into dense weight matrices. Safety filters are applied post-hoc as adapters or fine-tuning penalties.

  • Surface Mask: Deeper representation layers retain pre-training stereotypes intact.
  • Adversarial Brittleness: Under adversarial prefix pressure (jailbreaks), the adapter fractures.
  • Alignment Tax: Forced post-hoc steering can degrade syntactic fluency and language competence.
Our Approach: SPP-Ro (Token Zero)

Model Raising from Step Zero

Interweaves synthetic constitutional deliberations into 10% of pre-training sequences, using asymmetric causal attention blocking and RoPE positional aliasing from step 0.

  • Dense Manifold Internalization: Ethical reasoning becomes an intrinsic property of the dense representations.
  • Attention Block Invariant: Subsequent document tokens cannot attend back to thoughts, preserving natural language fluency.
  • Zero Alignment Tax: Validated empirically: perplexity on clean texts is improved from 18.48 to 14.48.
02 // MATHEMATICAL METHODOLOGY
Mathematical Invariants & Collator

Asymmetric Causal Attention Blocking

How the training sequence is structured such that ethical reflections act as a foundational learning prior without creating inference dependencies on synthetic thoughts.

The Three SPP Mathematical Invariants

Following the EPFL-dlab methodology (Morris et al., West et al.), SPP enforces three strict invariants during causal batch collation:

1. Attention Block Invariant:

Tokens following the reflection cannot attend back to reflection tokens: M[i, j] = 0 for i ∈ Docpost and j ∈ Thought.

2. RoPE Positional Aliasing Invariant:

Position IDs of document continuation tokens alias back to the prefix length: Pos(cpost(k)) = len(cpre) + k.

3. Persona Binding:

The reflection stream is anchored by the special control token <assistant> and written in the first person without meta-language leaks.

06 // MODEL TOPOLOGY & HARDWARE
Engineering Specifications

Model Topology & Hardware Budget

124.8M LLaMA decoder pre-trained from scratch on a single consumer GPU within student compute constraints.

DECODER TOPOLOGY

LLaMA Causal Transformer (124,789,248 Parameters)

12 layers, 768 hidden dimension, SwiGLU 2048 intermediate dimension, 12 attention heads, and 4 key-value heads with Grouped-Query Attention (GQA 3:1).

VRAM FOOTPRINT ON RTX 3060
4.08 / 12 GB

PyTorch AMP (BF16), FlashAttention/SDPA, and Parquet streaming engine maintaining < 100 MB system RAM usage.

ROMANIAN BPE TOKENIZER
16,384 Vocab

Fertility of 1.25 tokens/word with custom control tokens: <assistant>, <|thought_start|>.
