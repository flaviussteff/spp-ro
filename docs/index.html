01 // THE RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS
Theoretical Motivation

The Superficial Alignment Hypothesis

Do post-hoc techniques (SFT/RLHF) fundamentally excise bias from a language model, or do they simply teach it a polite conversational mask over toxic pre-trained representations?

Traditional Post-Hoc Alignment

Superficial Filter (SFT / RLHF)

Pre-trains on raw, biased web data for trillions of tokens. Safety and civic guardrails are bolted on later during fine-tuning.

  • Surface Mask: Internal token representations retain raw societal stereotypes.
  • Adversarial Fragility: Jailbreak prompts immediately collapse the polite conversational facade.
  • Language Disparity: Safety aligned in English fails significantly when probed in Romanian.
Our Approach: SPP-Ro

Model Raising from Token Zero

Interleaves constitutional self-reflections into 10% of training documents with causal attention blocking from the very first gradient update.

  • Deep Grounding: Ethical reasoning is baked into the model's fundamental causal weights.
  • Jailbreak Resilience: Retains constitutional dignity even under adversarial provocation.
  • Cross-Lingual Consistency: Fairness persists seamlessly across Romanian and English.
02 // CAUSAL ATTENTION BLOCKING & DATA
Methodology & Causal Mechanism

Data Streaming & Causal Attention Blocking

How 200 GB of Romanian text is curated, diacritic-standardized, and bifurcated to enforce constitutional deliberation without compromising text perplexity.

The 10% Reflection Split

In accordance with the EPFL SPP findings, annotating 100% of pre-training tokens causes severe perplexity collapse. We isolate exactly 10% of documents for constitutional thought injection:

Recent News Archive (2021–2026): 7% of SPP Pool

Grounds reflections in contemporary realities: Roma minority rights (§1.1), modern workplace gender parity (§1.2), and anti-disinformation (§2.3).

Wikipedia & General Knowledge: 93% of SPP Pool

Anchors moral persona in historical, scientific, philosophical, and European democratic foundations.

05 // HARDWARE & CAUSAL TOPOLOGY
Engineering Specifications

125M Consumer-Grade Topology

Chinchilla-compliant architecture engineered for single-GPU RTX 3060 12GB VRAM execution.

CORE ARCHITECTURE

Causal Decoder-Only Transformer

Modern Llama-3 style causal topology featuring SwiGLU non-linear gating, 12 layers, 12 query attention heads, and 768 hidden dimension (dmodel).

ATTENTION OPTIMIZATION
GQA 3:1

4 Key-Value heads drastically minimize KV-cache footprint during training and generation.

MEMORY ENVELOPE
< 9.5 GB

Operates comfortably within 12 GB GDDR6 using FlashAttention-2 / SDPA and Gradient Checkpointing.

CUSTOM TOKENIZER

16,384 Romanian BPE Vocab

Achieves fertility ratio of 1.25 tokens/word (vs 2.3 for Llama 3) with dedicated constitutional tokens: <|thought_start|> and <|thought_end|>.

POSITION ENCODING

Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE)

Relative positional awareness decoupled from absolute indices, ensuring seamless attention-blocking compatibility.
