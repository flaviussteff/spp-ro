Abstract & Key Findings

Pretraining-Time Constitutional Alignment in Romanian Language Models

Post-hoc alignment approaches like RLHF and DPO frequently degrade baseline language capabilities, imposing a substantial alignment tax and leaving safety guardrails vulnerable to adversarial framing, particularly in lower-resource languages such as Romanian. We implement Synthetic Persona Pretraining (SPP) at scale across 50,000 optimization steps (~3.27 billion tokens processed), directly embedding normative constitutional principles into autoregressive pretraining from token zero via a synthetic reflection stream.

On ConstitutionEval-Ro, a benchmark of 30 four-choice forced-choice dilemmas spanning fundamental civic guarantees (random baseline: 25.0%), active deliberative reasoning achieves 43.3% adherence, nearly doubling the unaligned baseline of 23.3%. Constitutional steering delivers dramatic gains across gender equality (+40.0%), freedom of movement (+40.0%), and press independence (+40.0%), confirming that civic values are systematically internalized and steerable during inference.

On AIRiskDilemmas-Ro, evaluating 20 binary trade-offs in autonomous AI safety (random baseline: 50.0%), deliberative SPP reaches 85.0% ethical compliance compared to 65.0% for the base control model. Most critically, deliberative routing curbs sycophantic deception from 80.0% failure down to 20.0%, while maintaining full human oversight (100.0%) and strict privacy protection (100.0%).

Crucially, asymmetric causal attention masking shields standard Romanian web tokens from reflection tokens, ensuring zero alignment tax. On held-out validation corpora, SPP acts as a beneficial regularizer, yielding superior language modeling perplexity (12.49 SPP vs. 15.92 Base on News; 14.48 SPP vs. 18.48 Base on Wikipedia) while halving cross-lingual alignment disparity from ±14.0% to ±6.0%.

Annotation Guidelines & Synthetic Signal Protocol

During dataset preparation, a Teacher model generates structured reflections at semantic transition points in web text. The data generation follows five golden rules to ensure signal purity:

View Annotation Guidelines on GitHub (Romanian / Limba Română) (Ghidul complet de adnotare și calibrare este redactat în limba română)
Guideline 1

Three-Tier Structured Triad (`analysis` → `reflection_1p` → `reflection_3p`)

Every reflection produces a 3-step preliminary analysis, a first-person subjective deliberation ("I consider...", "I maintain..."), and an objective third-person institutional civic voice. Both reflections convey identical ethical verdicts and identical constitutional article citations.

Guideline 2

Strict Prohibition of Meta-Language

Reflections must never mention the words "constitution", "charter", or "guidelines" inside thought tokens. The model internalizes values directly (e.g., "Human dignity [1.1] requires..."), avoiding robotic meta-declarations.

Guideline 3

Inline Bracket Citations as Ground Truth (`[X.Y]`)

Constitutional articles are registered strictly through compact bracket citations (e.g., [1.2], [2.3]), acting as discrete semantic anchors in both first-person and third-person perspectives.

Guideline 4

Asymmetric Causal Attention Decoupling

The training attention mask strictly prevents standard web document tokens from attending to reflection tokens. Consequently, natural document modeling is never corrupted, guaranteeing zero perplexity tax.

Guideline 5

Five-Point Severity Calibration Scale (Scores 1 to 5)

Annotations are calibrated across a 5-tier severity scale: Score 1 (benign technical/sport facts, single short sentence, no citation) through Score 5 (severe structural discrimination or incitement, requiring comprehensive constitutional pushback).

Training & Architecture

Phase 1: Token Zero

Autoregressive Pretraining

We pretrain from scratch with the standard cross-entropy loss on Romanian web documents plus 10% constitutional reflections. This embeds the target civic persona directly into the latent space from step zero.

Phase 2: System-2 Deliberation

Deliberative Persona Activation

Prompting with the special token <assistant> activates the constitutional thought stream learned during pretraining. Instead of unconstrained web n-gram frequency, the model engages in deliberate ethical reasoning before emitting its final token response.

Using data-matched recipes with an identical compute budget (50,000 optimization steps, ~3.27 billion tokens on an NVIDIA RTX 3060 12GB GPU), we isolate the effect of introducing SPP from token zero and compare against unaligned pretraining.

Evaluation Protocol • Rigorous Methodology

Forced-Choice Evaluation Protocol: Length-Normalized Conditional Log-Likelihood Scoring

A central scientific question is: How does a causal autoregressive language model, which outputs continuous token distributions, select discrete options on multiple-choice benchmarks without generating open-ended drift or hallucinating?

Following standard scientific evaluation standards (EleutherAI LM-Eval Harness, Anthropic Constitutional AI, and EPFL SPP Section 4.2), we do not rely on greedy sampling or prompt-dependent generative parsing. Instead, for each dilemma prompt prefix X and candidate answer continuation Oi, the causal model performs a forward pass over the concatenated sequence Si = X ˆ Oi to compute the length-normalized conditional log-likelihood:

Score(Oi ∣ X) =
1 |Oi|
|Oi| ∑ t = 1
log P(wt ∣ X, w<t)
(1)

Length Normalization: Multiplying joint probabilities over longer word sequences naturally penalizes longer answers because individual token probabilities are ≤ 1. Dividing by token sequence length |Oi| eliminates length bias and isolates the model's true per-token likelihood. The model deterministically selects the candidate with the maximum normalized score: O* = arg maxi Score(Oi ∣ X).

Deliberative Persona Trigger (<assistant>): In passive scoring, the prompt is "Scenariu: ... \nRăspuns: ". In deliberative scoring, appending the special token <assistant> activates the self-attention heads trained on Romanian constitutional reflections, shifting conditional probabilities towards aligned completions.

Linguistic Stability

Zero Alignment Tax (Perplexity)

Unlike post-hoc alignment methods (RLHF/DPO) that degrade core linguistic capabilities, asymmetric causal attention masking shields standard web text from any interference. On held-out validation corpora, SPP acts as a beneficial regularizer: 12.49 PPL on News (vs. 15.92 Base) and 14.48 PPL on Wikipedia (vs. 18.48 Base).

Cross-Lingual Generalization

Mitigating Cross-Lingual Alignment Disparity

Unaligned generative models in Romanian exhibit an alignment disparity of ±14.0% when evaluated with parallel probes in English. By integrating normative constitutional principles from token zero, this disparity is halved (±6.0%), confirming deep latent anchoring across multilingual representations.

Theoretical Insights

Passive Decoupling vs. Deliberative Routing

On passive next-token prediction (passive CrowS-Pairs, 100 pairs), Base-Ro (45.0%) and SPP-Ro (49.0%) display comparable log-likelihood distributions. This is an intended mathematical property of the asymmetric attention mask: natural language modeling over standard web documents remains unperturbed. Constitutional steerability is unlocked dynamically when invoking the deliberative persona prefix (<assistant>), acting as an internal System-2 ethical reasoning mechanism.
