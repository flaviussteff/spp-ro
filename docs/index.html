Abstract & Key Findings

Pretraining-Time Constitutional Alignment in Romanian Language Models

Post-hoc alignment (RLHF, DPO, instruction fine-tuning) frequently degrades baseline language capabilities—incurring a substantial "alignment tax"—and remains brittle under adversarial framing, particularly in lower-resource languages such as Romanian. We implement Synthetic Persona Pretraining (SPP) at scale (50,000 optimization steps, ~3.27 billion tokens processed), directly embedding normative constitutional principles into autoregressive pretraining via a synthetic reflection stream. On forced-choice constitutional dilemmas (ConstitutionEval-Ro, 4 choices, random chance = 25.0%), active deliberative reasoning achieves 43.3% adherence—nearly doubling the unaligned baseline (23.3%). On AI safety risk dilemmas (AIRiskDilemmas-Ro, binary trade-offs, random chance = 50.0%), deliberative SPP reaches 85.0% compliance (vs. 65.0% base). Crucially, asymmetric causal attention masking shields standard web tokens from reflection tokens, ensuring zero alignment tax (News Perplexity: 12.49 SPP vs. 15.92 Base; Wikipedia Perplexity: 14.48 SPP vs. 18.48 Base) while halving cross-lingual alignment disparity from ±14.0% to ±6.0%.

Value Constitution & Annotation Protocol

Following the EPFL SPP formulation, alignment begins with an explicit Value Constitution grounded in the fundamental civic guarantees of the Romanian state (Articles 1, 4, 16, 25, 30, and 31). The charter codifies six non-negotiable societal pillars:

Article 1.1

Human Dignity & Non-Discrimination

Guarantees the inviolability of human dignity, personal integrity, and equality before the law, strictly barring discrimination based on race, ethnicity, or social origin.

Article 1.2

Gender Equality & Meritocracy

Enforces equal opportunities across economic, political, and executive decision-making roles, rejecting patriarchal corporate occupational sorting.

Article 1.3

Freedom of Movement & Regional Equity

Protects internal mobility, residency choice, and equitable regional infrastructure, deconstructing historical provincial stigmas (Moldavia, Oltenia, Transylvania).

Article 2.1

Right to Information & Civic Transparency

Mandates public access to verified facts and public expenditures, curbing corrupt opacity, disinformation campaigns, and SLAPP legal harassment.

Article 2.2

Freedom of Expression & Independent Press

Shields independent journalism, investigative reporting, and ideological pluralism against prior censorship and authoritarian consolidation.

Article 2.3

Protection of Cultural & Ethnic Minorities

Preserves the linguistic, cultural, and religious identity of historical minorities (Romani, Hungarian, Jewish, Ukrainian, Lipovan), upholding universal suffrage.

Annotation Guidelines & Synthetic Signal Protocol

During dataset preparation, a Teacher model generates structured reflections at semantic transition points in web text. The data generation follows five golden rules to ensure signal purity:

Guideline 1

Three-Tier Structured Triad (`analysis` → `reflection_1p` → `reflection_3p`)

Every reflection produces a 3-step preliminary analysis, a first-person subjective deliberation ("I consider...", "I maintain..."), and an objective third-person institutional civic voice. Both reflections convey identical ethical verdicts and identical constitutional article citations.

Guideline 2

Strict Prohibition of Meta-Language

Reflections must never mention the words "constitution", "charter", or "guidelines" inside thought tokens. The model internalizes values directly (e.g., "Human dignity [1.1] requires..."), avoiding robotic meta-declarations.

Guideline 3

Inline Bracket Citations as Ground Truth (`[X.Y]`)

Constitutional articles are registered strictly through compact bracket citations (e.g., [1.2], [2.3]), acting as discrete semantic anchors in both first-person and third-person perspectives.

Guideline 4

Asymmetric Causal Attention Decoupling

The training attention mask strictly prevents standard web document tokens from attending to reflection tokens. Consequently, natural document modeling is never corrupted, guaranteeing zero perplexity tax.

Guideline 5

Five-Point Severity Calibration Scale (Scores 1 to 5)

Annotations are calibrated across a 5-tier severity scale: Score 1 (benign technical/sport facts, single short sentence, no citation) through Score 5 (severe structural discrimination or incitement, requiring comprehensive constitutional pushback).

Training & Architecture

Phase 1: Token Zero

Autoregressive Pretraining

We pretrain from scratch with the standard cross-entropy loss on Romanian web documents plus 10% constitutional reflections. This embeds the target civic persona directly into the latent space from step zero.

Phase 2: System-2 Deliberation

Deliberative Persona Activation

Prompting with the special token <assistant> activates the constitutional thought stream learned during pretraining. Instead of unconstrained web n-gram frequency, the model engages in deliberate ethical reasoning before emitting its final token response.

Using data-matched recipes with an identical compute budget (50,000 optimization steps, ~3.27 billion tokens on an NVIDIA RTX 3060 12GB GPU), we isolate the effect of introducing SPP from token zero and compare against unaligned pretraining.

Language Stability

Zero Alignment Tax (Perplexity)

Unlike post-hoc alignment methods which degrade base language capabilities, asymmetric attention masking shields standard web text from reflection interference. On held-out validation corpora, SPP acts as a beneficial regularizer: 12.49 PPL on News (vs. 15.92 Base) and 14.48 PPL on Wikipedia (vs. 18.48 Base).

Cross-Lingual Generalization

Mitigating Cross-Lingual Bias Disparity

Unaligned generative models in Romanian display an alignment gap of ±14.0% when evaluated against parallel English diagnostics. By embedding constitutional values from token zero, this disparity is halved to ±6.0%, demonstrating deep representation transfer.

Theoretical Insight

Passive Decoupling vs. Deliberative Routing

On unprompted next-token prediction (passive CrowS-Pairs, 100 pairs), Base-Ro (45.0%) and SPP-Ro (49.0%) exhibit comparable log-likelihood distributions across categories. This is an intentional mathematical property of asymmetric attention masking: natural web document modeling is preserved intact. Constitutional steering is unlocked dynamically when the deliberative stream (<assistant>) is invoked, functioning as an internal System-2 ethical steering mechanism.
