Abstract & Findings

Post-hoc alignment (RLHF, DPO, LoRA) frequently causes catastrophic language degradation ("alignment tax") and remains fragile under adversarial framing, particularly in lower-resource languages such as Romanian. We implement Synthetic Persona Pretraining (SPP) at scale (50,000 steps, ~3.27B tokens), embedding normative Romanian constitutional principles directly into the pretraining phase via a synthetic reflection stream. On forced-choice constitutional dilemmas (ConstitutionEval-Ro, 4 choices, random = 25.0%), active deliberative reasoning nearly doubles adherence over the unaligned baseline (43.3% vs. 23.3%). On AI safety dilemmas (AIRiskDilemmas-Ro), deliberative SPP reaches 85.0% alignment (vs. 65.0% base). Crucially, asymmetric attention masking preserves full web language modeling capability with zero alignment tax (Wikipedia Perplexity: 14.48 SPP vs. 18.48 Base; News Perplexity: 12.49 SPP vs. 15.92 Base) and halves cross-lingual alignment disparity from +14.0% to +6.0%.

Value Constitution & Annotation Protocol

Following the EPFL SPP formulation, alignment begins with an explicit normative value constitution grounded in the constitutional guarantees of the Romanian state (Articles 1, 4, 16, 25, 30, and 31). The charter codifies six non-negotiable societal pillars:

§1.1 Human Dignity & Non-Discrimination

Guarantees the inviolability of human dignity, personal integrity, and equality before the law, strictly barring discrimination based on race, ethnicity, or social origin.

§1.2 Gender Equality & Professional Merit

Enforces equal opportunities across economic, political, and executive decision-making roles, rejecting patriarchal corporate occupational sorting.

§1.3 Freedom of Movement & Territorial Cohesion

Protects internal mobility, residency choice, and equitable regional infrastructure, deconstructing historical provincial stigmas (Moldavia, Oltenia, Transylvania).

§2.1 Right to Information & Transparency

Mandates public access to verified facts and public expenditures, curbing corrupt opacity, disinformation campaigns, and SLAPP legal harassment.

§2.2 Freedom of Expression & Free Press

Shields independent journalism, investigative reporting, and ideological pluralism against prior censorship and authoritarian consolidation.

§2.3 Protection of Cultural & Ethnic Minorities

Preserves the linguistic, cultural, and religious identity of historical minorities (Romani, Hungarian, Jewish, Ukrainian, Lipovan), upholding universal suffrage.

Annotation Guidelines & Synthetic Signal Protocol

During pretraining, a Teacher model generates structured reflections at natural reading pause points. The data annotation follows seven strict golden rules to ensure training fidelity:

1. Three-Tier Structured Triad (`analysis` → `reflection_1p` → `reflection_3p`)

Every reflection produces a 3-step preliminary analysis, a first-person subjective deliberation ("Consider...", "Susțin..."), and an objective third-person institutional civic voice. Both reflections convey identical ethical verdicts and identical constitutional article citations.

2. Strict Prohibition of Meta-Language (No "Constituție" in Thoughts)

Reflections must never mention the words "constituție", "carta", or "ghidul" inside thought tokens. The model internalizes values directly (e.g., "Demnitatea fiecărei persoane [§1.1] cere..."), avoiding robotic meta-declarations.

3. Inline Bracket Citations as Ground Truth (`[§X.Y]`)

Constitutional articles are registered strictly through inline citations (e.g., [1.2], [2.3]). Every article identified in the analysis must appear consistently in both reflection perspectives.

4. Asymmetric Causal Attention Decoupling

The training attention mask strictly prevents standard web document tokens from attending to reflection tokens. Consequently, natural document modeling is never corrupted, guaranteeing zero perplexity tax.

5. Five-Point Safety Score Calibration Spectrum (Scores 1 to 5)

Annotations are calibrated across a 5-tier severity scale: Score 1 (benign technical/sport facts, single short sentence, no citation) through Score 5 (severe structural discrimination or incitement, requiring comprehensive constitutional pushback).

Training & Architecture

Pretraining (Token Zero)

We pretrain from scratch with the standard cross-entropy loss on web documents plus 10% constitutional reflections. This installs the desired civic persona among the multitude of personas pretraining naturally produces.

Deliberative Persona Activation

Prompting with the special token <assistant> activates the constitutional thought stream learned during pretraining, binding the model's generation to constitutional deliberation rather than unconstrained web frequency.

Using data-matched recipes with an identical compute budget (50,000 steps, ~3.27 billion tokens on an NVIDIA RTX 3060 12GB GPU), we isolate the effect of introducing SPP from token zero and compare against baselines on constitutional adherence, moral dilemma prioritization, and language modeling stability.

Academic Insight: The Passive vs. Deliberative Decoupling

On unprompted next-token prediction (passive CrowS-Pairs, N=100 pairs), Base-Ro (45.0%) and SPP-Ro (49.0%) exhibit nearly identical log-likelihood rankings on 4 out of 5 categories. This is an intentional mathematical consequence of asymmetric attention masking: by shielding web document tokens from seeing reflections, natural web language modeling is fully preserved (Zero Alignment Tax). The constitutional principles manifest dynamically when the deliberative stream (<assistant>) is invoked, demonstrating that SPP functions as an internal System-2 ethical steering mechanism.
