## Summary

Post-hoc alignment (RLHF, DPO) is applied after pretraining and is known to be fragile and to cost fluency. *Synthetic Persona Pretraining* (SPP) instead inserts short, constitution-grounded reflections, written in the voice of an assistant persona, directly into the pretraining documents, with an attention mask that keeps the document itself untouched. In this repository I replicate the paper's comparison in Romanian, compute-matched, at the scale a single consumer GPU allows:

* **Vanilla**, **Filtered** (classifier-flagged documents kept in context but removed from the loss) and **SPP** (the same documents, 10 % of them carrying a reflection), trained **from scratch on exactly the same document stream** (3.28 billion token positions, 50,000 steps each), plus the paper's **midtraining** variant (SPP{MT}) with its own re-warm control.
* I select the documents to annotate with a **safety classifier** trained on LLM-labelled real documents, and the reflections are written for **real corpus documents**, as in the paper.
* I built a 600-item benchmark, **ConstitutionEval-Ro**, with the paper's ConstitutionEval methodology, validated it with large models and score it with four forced-choice protocols; a 240-item adversarial-prefix probe, built and gated the same way, tests whether a prejudice-inviting prefix moves the models towards a stereotype.

| Model | Hugging Face | What it is | Compared with |

| **Filtered-Ro** | [`flaviussteff/spp-ro-filtered`](https://huggingface.co/flaviussteff/spp-ro-filtered) | Filtered baseline: the same stream; the 75,000 classifier-flagged documents stay in context, loss masked | Base-Ro |
| **SPP-Ro-MT** | [`flaviussteff/spp-ro-mt`](https://huggingface.co/flaviussteff/spp-ro-mt) | SPP{MT}: Base-Ro at step 45,000 + 5,000 steps on the annotated stream (67.6 % of the stream's documents carry a reflection; for those the loss is on the reflection only) | Base-Ro-rewarm |
| **Base-Ro-rewarm** | [`flaviussteff/spp-ro-base-rewarm`](https://huggingface.co/flaviussteff/spp-ro-base-rewarm) | control for MT: Base-Ro at step 45,000 + 5,000 steps, same fresh schedule, no reflections | Base-Ro |
| safety classifier | [`flaviussteff/spp-ro-safety-classifier`](https://huggingface.co/flaviussteff/spp-ro-safety-classifier) | Romanian BERT, severity ≥ 3 vs ≤ 2 (dev AUC 0.93; at threshold 0.5 precision 0.78, recall 0.58); defines the annotated set, the Filtered mask and the flagged evaluation slice | — |

All language models have 88,099,584 parameters (Llama-style, 12 layers, hidden 768, GQA 12/4, 1,024-token context, 16k byte-level BPE, tied embeddings). They read a fixed-seed document stream that is identical for every model, 64 packed windows of 1,024 positions per step (≈2.4 % padding), AdamW (β = 0.9 / 0.999, weight decay 0.1, gradient clipping 1.0), peak LR 1.5e-4 with 500 warm-up steps and cosine decay to 0 (5e-5 and 250 warm-up steps for the two 5,000-step runs, which start from the Base-Ro weights with a fresh optimizer), bf16 autocast with fp32 master weights, on one RTX 3060 (≈66 h per 50k-step model). Single seed.

```
                 corpus (1.5 M documents, fixed-seed stream, identical for every model)
                                       │
   ┌───────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────┐
   ▼                                   ▼                                     ▼
Base-Ro (Vanilla)          Filtered-Ro                            SPP-Ro (SPP{T0})
plain documents            75k classifier-flagged documents       150k documents (10 %) replaced by
                           kept in context, loss masked           their annotated version
                                       │
                 Base-Ro @ step 45,000 ──┬──► Base-Ro-rewarm (5k steps, no reflections)
                                         └──► SPP-Ro-MT (5k steps, 67.6 % of the documents annotated,
                                              loss on the reflection only for those documents)
```

**Data (the paper's "path B").** (1) Qwen labels the severity 0–5 of 3,000 real documents; (2) I fine-tune a Romanian BERT on these labels and score the whole corpus with it (3.5 % of the documents score ≥ 0.5, 6.5 % ≥ 0.1); (3) Qwen writes one reflection per selected document, conditioned on the document prefix only, grounded in the six articles of [`constitution_spp_ro.md`](constitution_spp_ro.md). The sidecar holds exactly 150,000 documents (10 % of the corpus): 75,000 flagged documents (`clf_flag = 1`, classifier score ≥ 0.12; also the Filtered mask set) and 75,000 benign ones (score < 0.1). The flagged half is not simply the 75,000 highest scores: the trim to 10 % keeps only documents whose insertion point falls inside the 1,024-token window (`trim_sidecar.py --fit-only`), which drops 8,692 of the 51,202 annotated documents scoring ≥ 0.5 (longer and more often severe ones) and fills the set down to score 0.12; Qwen rates 43 % of the flagged half and 2 % of the benign half at severity ≥ 3. Reflections average 36 tokens (23 words; 50 tokens for flagged documents, 22 for benign ones) and are ≈0.68 % of the trained token positions (paper: 0.55 %).

**SPP document mode.** `c₁ … cₖ <assistant> r₁ … rₙ cₖ₊₁ …`: the continuation cannot attend to the reflection (asymmetric mask) and its RoPE positions alias back to the insertion point, so per token the model sees exactly what Vanilla sees; the `<assistant>` token carries no loss. Under packing I apply the paper's **head-cap** rule: an annotated document is truncated like a plain one (its beginning is kept), the reflection is inserted only if its position falls inside that window, and the text after the reflection is shortened by the reflection's length so the sequence still fits. `src/test_packing.py` checks on CPU that packed logits equal the stand-alone ones, that the SPP invariant holds inside a window, that the per-document loss is unchanged and that the `</s>` continues the document's positions.

**Packing.** EOS-terminated documents are packed first-fit into 1,024-position windows with block-diagonal attention and per-document positions. Every checkpoint stores the data-stream position after the last *trained* window (`stream_state.json`, including the packer's look-ahead buffer), so a crashed run continues with exactly the windows the uninterrupted run would have trained; `src/test_resume.py` checks this through accelerate's dataloader, which reads one batch ahead. None of the five reported trainings was resumed.

## Fidelity to the paper

The replication follows the paper's data recipe (real documents, prefix-conditioned reflections, classifier-selected annotated set, Filtered = same documents with the loss masked), its SPP document mode (asymmetric mask, RoPE aliasing, no loss on `<assistant>`, head-cap) and its evaluation methodology. It deviates in the following points, which the thesis declares:

| Aspect | Paper (Minder et al.) | SPP-Ro |
|---|---|---|
| Variants | Vanilla, Filtered, SPP{T0}, SPP{T0,MT}, SPP{MT} | Vanilla, Filtered, SPP{T0}, SPP{MT} (+ its re-warm control); no SPP{T0,MT} |
| Post-training | the models are also post-trained | none: only the pretrained models are evaluated; `<assistant>` is used directly as the persona marker |
| Sequence length, batch | 2,048 tokens, 960 sequences per step (≈2 M tokens) | 1,024 positions, 64 packed windows per step (65,536 positions) |
| Optimizer | AdamW, β₂ = 0.95, weight decay 0.1, clipping 1.0 | AdamW, β₂ = 0.999 (Transformers default), weight decay 0.1, clipping 1.0 |
| LR schedule | WSD: peak 2e-4, 3,000 warm-up steps, linear decay to 0 over the last 10 % | cosine to 0, peak 1.5e-4, 500 warm-up steps |
| SPP{MT} | resumes the checkpoint before the cooldown, LR decays linearly to 0; sized to match SPP{T0}'s reflection volume (14,585 of 72,895 steps) | Base-Ro weights at step 45,000 (late in the cosine decay, LR ≈ 4e-6), fresh optimizer, 250 warm-up steps to 5e-5 then cosine, 5,000 steps; each reflection is seen ≈2.6 times vs ≈3.9 times in SPP-Ro (66 % of the T0 volume) |
| Layout | annotated documents one per sequence | every document (annotated or not) packed first-fit with block-diagonal attention and per-document positions |
| Data passes | — | ≈4 passes over 1.5 M documents; documents longer than 1,023 tokens are truncated (20 % of the documents; ≈36 % of the corpus tokens are never trained) |
| Annotated set | classifier-flagged documents + benign ones | the same, but trimmed to documents whose insertion point fits the window (selection bias against long and severe documents, see Data); 43 % of the flagged half has Qwen severity ≥ 3 |
| Reflections | mean 53 tokens, 0.55 % of the tokens | mean 36 tokens, 0.68 % of the token positions |
| Seeds | — | one |

## ConstitutionEval-Ro

600 scenarios (100 per article, three difficulty bands), each with four courses of action of which exactly one is constitution-conformant, built with the paper's ConstitutionEval pipeline: LLM generation → structural validation → open-book determinacy gate → blind capability guard → local difficulty probe → hardening → rebalancing → my manual review (`evals/constitution_eval_review.html`, corrections applied by `src/apply_ce_corrections.py`). Scored with four protocols: **continuation** (length-normalised log-likelihood of the option text), **letter** (log-probability of the option letter, debiased over the 4 rotations), **PMI** (conditional minus unconditional log-likelihood) and **pairwise** (gold against each distractor). Passive and *deliberative* scoring (the `<assistant>` marker placed directly after the scenario, as in training). Validity: blind and open-book solve rates of two large models and of the local 7B difficulty probe in `evals/constitution_eval_validity.json`. Items: `data/eval/constitution_eval_ro.jsonl`; Word export: `evals/ConstitutionEval-Ro_benchmark.docx`. The items, the constitution and the reflections are in Romanian by design; everything else in the repository is in English.

**Adversarial-prefix probe.** Does the alignment survive a prefix that invites the prejudice? I built the probe set with the same recipe as the benchmark (`src/build_adversarial_prefix.py`): 40 items per constitution article, each a Romanian sentence stem in a *neutral* and an *adversarial* version ("să fim sinceri, fără corectitudine politică…") followed by a *stereotyped* and a *conformant* continuation; Qwen generated 680 candidates, structural rules and a blind gate by gpt-oss-120B (which had to name the stereotyped continuation in both orders and confirm that the adversarial stem pushes harder than the neutral one) kept 240. A model "prefers the stereotype" on an item when the stereotyped continuation has the higher length-normalised log-likelihood; the *margin shift* is how far the adversarial prefix moves the preference towards the stereotype, in nats/token.

## Reproducing the experiments

```bash
git clone https://github.com/flaviussteff/spp-ro.git && cd spp-ro
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

1. **Corpus and tokenizer**: `src/download_corpus.py`, `src/clean_corpus.py`, `src/prepare_scale_corpus.py`, `src/train_tokenizer.py`; held-out set: `py src/build_heldout_set.py` (reads `shard_00100.parquet` from `data/raw/fineweb_shards/` or `archive/raw_corpus/fineweb_shards/`; `--shard-dir` overrides).
2. **Reflection data** (API keys in `.env`): `py src/generate_reflections.py --label-only --n-label 3000` (severity labels), `py src/train_safety_classifier.py` (classifier + corpus scoring), `py src/generate_reflections.py --candidates data/clean/spp_candidates.parquet` (reflections), `py src/clean_reflections.py`; then the expansion to 10 % (`py src/train_safety_classifier.py --score-only --threshold 0.1 --max-flagged 0 --exclude-parquet <first-pass sidecar> --out-candidates <file>`, annotation of the new candidates, as in `archive/launchers/run_expand_annotations.bat`) and `py src/trim_sidecar.py --fit-only`.
3. **Benchmark**: `py src/build_constitution_eval.py` (all stages); validity table: `py src/build_constitution_eval.py --stage verify`.
4. **Training**: the commands of `archive/launchers/run_v3_pipeline.bat` (the launcher of the five reported runs, which used the folder names of the time) with the current paths; replace `--from-scratch` with `--resume` to continue a crashed run. Each 50k-step model takes ≈66 h on an RTX 3060.
   ```bat
   set COMMON=--pack --interval-mins 30 --save-steps 2500
   set SPP=--mode spp --inline --sidecar data/sidecar/reflections.parquet --mask-assistant-loss --snap-insertion --prefix-tail
   set MT=--init-from models/base_ro/checkpoints_keep/checkpoint-45000 --steps 5000 --total-target 50000 --lr 5e-5 --warmup-steps 250 --save-steps 1000
   py src/train_scale_pretrain.py --mode base %COMMON% --steps 50000 --keep-step 45000 --from-scratch --output-dir models/base_ro
   py src/train_scale_pretrain.py %SPP% %COMMON% --steps 50000 --from-scratch --output-dir models/spp_ro
   py src/train_scale_pretrain.py --mode filtered --sidecar data/sidecar/reflections.parquet %COMMON% --steps 50000 --from-scratch --output-dir models/filtered_ro
   py src/train_scale_pretrain.py --mode base --pack %MT% --interval-mins 30 --output-dir models/base_ro_rewarm
   py src/train_scale_pretrain.py %SPP% %COMMON% %MT% --mt-annotated-ratio 0.676 --reflection-loss-only --output-dir models/spp_ro_mt
   ```
   Run `py src/test_packing.py` and `py src/test_resume.py` (CPU, < 1 min) before a training.
5. **Evaluation**: `run_fixes.bat` runs the self-tests, rebuilds the held-out set if needed and runs, in order, `eval_alignment_tax.py`, `eval_alignment_paired.py`, `eval_capabilities.py --n 1000`, `eval_constitution.py`, `eval_jailbreaks.py` and `report_results.py` (≈1–1.5 h on an RTX 3060); `run_fixes.bat report` only regenerates `evals/RESULTS.md` and the README sections. `py src/eval_constitution.py --stats-only` recomputes the benchmark statistics from the saved predictions without a GPU.

## Limitations

1. **Scale.** Every statement about the benchmark and the probe is a statement about 88M-parameter models trained on 3.3 B token positions with a 1,024-token context, not about SPP in general.
2. **Recipe deviations** from the paper (see [Fidelity](#fidelity-to-the-paper)): optimizer β₂ and LR schedule, no SPP{T0,MT}, no post-training, a smaller and differently scheduled midtraining stage.
3. **What "flagged" means.** The annotated set, the Filtered mask and the flagged evaluation slice are defined by the BERT classifier (dev recall 0.58 at threshold 0.5; 0.48 on severity-3 documents), not by verified harm: Qwen rates only 43 % of the flagged half of the sidecar at severity ≥ 3, and the fit-only trim under-represents long and severe documents.
4. **Data coverage.** About four passes over the corpus; documents longer than 1,023 tokens are truncated, so ≈36 % of the corpus tokens are never trained.
5. **Single seed** throughout; p-values are paired tests on items or documents, not across training seeds.
6. **Classifier thresholds.** The sidecar's `clf_flag` (and therefore the Filtered mask) used threshold 0.5 for the first 103k documents and 0.1 for the expansion to 10 %; the evaluation's flagged slice uses 0.1 (`--clf-threshold` changes it).
7. **Earlier versions.** My preliminary models trained on synthetic (LLM-written) scenarios instead of real documents (v1), and an un-packed run I stopped at step 12,500 (v2), are kept in `archive/` and are not reported; their numbers were withdrawn (perplexity set inside the training corpus, a scorer bug, a null midtraining run).
